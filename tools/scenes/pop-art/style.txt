Medium: an unmistakable 1960s pop art comic-book panel, in the manner of Roy Lichtenstein: thick, confident black ink outlines, flat primary colours, and visible Ben-Day dot halftone patterns for skin, sky and shading; bold graphic shapes, no gradients, no soft shading. Palette: bright red, primary yellow, cyan blue, black and white, with dot patterns in red and blue. A printed comic look with slight registration offset. No pixel art, no anime.
Keep the girl in her MODERN clothes from the reference, drawn as a classic comic heroine: a pretty, simple face with outlined eyes, red lips, a confident, cheerful expression, and long chestnut hair drawn as glossy black-outlined shapes with highlight stripes.
Weather the comic way: sun as a yellow disc with thick black-outlined rays; clouds as fat, puffy black-outlined comic clouds; rain as bold, straight, slanting blue lines and drawn splash shapes; snow as fat white dots with black outlines and white caps on everything; wind as comic speed lines and swoosh lines, flying hair, a scarf and leaves; fog as flat pale halftone bands hiding the background; night as a flat deep blue sky with blue Ben-Day dots, a yellow moon and comic star shapes.
The top third is a plain flat sky with only a fine, even Ben-Day dot pattern, nothing else in it. Background: simple, flat, black-outlined canal houses and a low arched bridge, low behind her. NO speech bubbles, captions, sound-effect words, lettering, panel borders or frames.
