Medium: an unmistakable 1960s pop art comic-book panel, in the manner of Roy Lichtenstein: thick, confident black ink outlines, flat primary colours, and visible Ben-Day dot halftone patterns for skin, sky and shading; bold graphic shapes, no gradients, no soft shading. Palette: bright red, primary yellow, cyan blue, black and white, with dot patterns in red and blue. A printed comic look with slight registration offset. No pixel art, no anime.
Keep the girl in her MODERN clothes from the reference, drawn as a classic 1960s romance-comic heroine like Lichtenstein's "Girl with Ball" and "Drowning Girl", NOT anime or chibi: no big eyes, no round baby face, no oversized head; a woman in her twenties with natural adult proportions, a pretty, simple face with heavy black-outlined almond eyes, long lashes, arched brows, a small nose drawn with one line, glossy red lips, skin in red Ben-Day dots, a confident, cheerful expression, and long chestnut hair drawn as glossy black-outlined shapes with highlight stripes.
Weather the comic way: sun as a yellow disc with thick black-outlined rays; clouds as fat, puffy black-outlined comic clouds; rain as bold, straight, slanting blue lines and drawn splash shapes; snow as fat white dots with black outlines and white caps on everything; wind as comic speed lines and swoosh lines, flying hair, a scarf and leaves; fog as flat pale halftone bands hiding the background; night as a flat deep blue sky with blue Ben-Day dots, a yellow moon and comic star shapes.
The top third is a plain flat sky with only a fine, even Ben-Day dot pattern, nothing else in it. Composition the Lichtenstein way, NOT a detailed city: she is the whole panel; behind her almost nothing but flat colour fields and Ben-Day dots, with at most one bold graphic hint of place low behind her: a flat strip of sea and a flat sandy beach with one or two simple dunes, as in "Girl with Ball". No detailed houses, no bridge, no flowers, no street lamps. NO speech bubbles, captions, sound-effect words, lettering, panel borders or frames.
