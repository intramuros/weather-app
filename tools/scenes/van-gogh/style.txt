Medium: an unmistakable oil painting by Vincent van Gogh, like "The Starry Night", "Café Terrace at Night" and his Arles canvases: thick impasto paint applied in short, directional, rhythmic brushstrokes that follow every form, visible ridges of paint, outlines in dark Prussian blue. His vivid palette: cobalt and ultramarine blue, chrome yellow, warm orange, viridian and emerald green, touches of red, cream. Every surface is made of visible strokes, but the girl stays calm and clearly readable, built from smoother, simpler strokes than the background. No pixel art, no anime shading, no photograph.
The girl is NOT cute, chibi or anime: no big eyes, no round baby face, no oversized head. Paint her the way van Gogh paints women in "L'Arlésienne", "La Mousmé" and "Marguerite Gachet at the Piano": a young woman in her twenties with natural adult proportions (head about one seventh of her height), a long face modelled in visible strokes of green, ochre and rose, a firm Prussian-blue contour around her face and figure, small dark eyes set under straight brows, a strong straight nose, a small closed mouth, a calm, thoughtful, slightly reserved expression, a natural, slightly turned pose. Keep her MODERN clothes from the reference, built from directional strokes that follow the folds, and her long chestnut hair as thick, flowing strokes of sienna and orange.
Weather the van Gogh way: sun as a blazing yellow disc ringed with concentric strokes; clouds as swirling, coiling strokes; rain as long, slanting, broken blue strokes; snow as thick dabs of white paint and white caps on everything; wind as streaming strokes, flying hair, a scarf and leaves; fog as soft, pale, horizontal strokes hiding the background; night as a deep blue swirling sky with glowing yellow stars and moon ringed in halos, like "The Starry Night".
The top third stays calm: a simple field of sky strokes in one colour family, with no swirls, stars or clouds above 33% of the height. Setting from his Arles canvases, NOT a city canal: she stands on a dirt path beside a country canal, with his wooden Langlois drawbridge small and low in the distance, golden wheat fields and a few sunflowers low beside her, flat fields to a low horizon (bare or white fields in cold and snowy scenes). No canal houses, no stone arched bridge, no paving, no tulips, no street lamps, poles or other tall dark shapes. No signature.
