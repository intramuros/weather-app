Traditional woodblock palette only: Prussian blue, indigo, vermilion, ochre, muted sage green, soft grey and warm off-white paper; visible washi paper grain and slight woodblock registration. Keep the girl in her MODERN clothes from the reference (not a kimono), drawn with ukiyo-e outline and flat colour, with a cute, gentle face and chestnut hair. No pixel art, no anime shading.
Weather the woodblock way: sun as a flat red-orange disc; clouds as flat stylised ribbons; rain as many fine, straight, parallel dark lines over the whole lower two thirds, as in Hiroshige's "Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi"; snow as round white dots against a grey sky with white snow caps on everything; wind as bending lines, a flying scarf and leaves; fog as flat pale bands; night as a deep indigo sky with a pale yellow moon.
Background: very sparse, like the empty space in a Hiroshige print: one or two flat, pale, simplified canal houses and a low arched bridge over flat water, nothing else (no flowers, no trees full of blossom). NO cartouche, title box, seal, signature or characters of any script.
