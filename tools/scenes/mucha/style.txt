Medium: an unmistakable Art Nouveau poster by Alphonse Mucha, like "The Seasons", "Zodiac" and his Job and Monaco–Monte-Carlo posters: confident, flowing dark brown contour lines of varying weight, flat areas of soft colour with only gentle tonal modelling, a lithograph's slightly grainy paper. Mucha's muted palette: sage and olive green, dusty rose, ochre and gold, warm cream, soft terracotta and powder blue, with dark brown lines.
The girl is NOT cute or childlike: she is a graceful, elegant young woman as Mucha painted her, with a serene, slightly dreamy expression, natural adult proportions and a calm, flowing pose. Her long, slightly wavy chestnut hair is drawn in Mucha's sweeping, stylised curls that move with the weather. Keep her MODERN clothes from the reference (not a robe or gown), drawn with Mucha's flowing folds.
Behind her head and shoulders, Mucha's signature large pale circular halo, a simple flat disc with a thin double outline and very faint ornament, its top edge no higher than 33% of the height. Stylised, flat Art Nouveau plant ornament (leaves, stems, flowers) may frame her lower body and the ground, but never as a border around the picture.
Weather the Art Nouveau way: sun as a flat golden disc with fine radiating lines; clouds as flat, stylised swirling ribbons; rain as fine, straight, slanting lines; snow as flat round white dots and white caps on everything; wind as flowing hair, a streaming scarf and swirling leaves in whiplash curves; fog as flat pale bands; night as a deep blue-green sky with a pale gold moon and small stars.
Background: very sparse and pale: one or two flat, simplified Amsterdam canal houses and a low arched bridge over flat water, in soft colours, low behind her.
