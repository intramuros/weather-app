Medium: an unmistakable Art Nouveau poster by Alphonse Mucha, like "The Seasons", "Zodiac" and his Job and Monaco–Monte-Carlo posters: confident, flowing dark brown contour lines of varying weight, flat areas of soft colour with only gentle tonal modelling, a lithograph's slightly grainy paper. Mucha's muted palette: sage and olive green, dusty rose, ochre and gold, warm cream, soft terracotta and powder blue, with dark brown lines.
The girl is NOT cute, childlike or anime: no big eyes, no manga or anime face. She is a woman in her twenties with the face of Mucha's models: a long oval face with East Asian features, a softly defined nose, a small closed mouth, almond-shaped, heavy-lidded eyes that are SMALL and realistic, gazing calmly down or to the side rather than at us, softly shaded cheeks, a serene, dreamy, slightly melancholic expression. Natural adult proportions (head about one seventh of her height), a calm, flowing, slightly turned pose. Her long dark brunette hair is a heavy, voluminous mass that unwinds into Mucha's sweeping, stylised whiplash curls moving with the weather. Keep her MODERN clothes from the reference (not a robe or gown), drawn with Mucha's flowing folds.
Behind her head and shoulders, Mucha's signature large pale circular halo, a simple flat disc with a thin double outline and very faint ornament, its top edge no higher than 33% of the height. Rich Mucha decoration fills the band from 35% of the height down to the ground: dense, stylised, flat Art Nouveau plant ornament (curling stems, leaves and flowers in whiplash curves, outlined in dark brown) rising from the ground on both sides of her and framing her lower body, and the weather's flat swirling ribbon clouds curling on both sides of the halo. This decoration is what makes it a Mucha poster, so be generous with it there, but never as a border around the picture.
The top third (0-33% of the height) is plain, flat cream paper or flat sky colour: no clouds, swirls, leaves, ornament or lines there at all. Weather the Art Nouveau way, all of it below 35% of the height: sun as a flat golden disc with fine radiating lines; clouds as flat, stylised swirling ribbons; rain as fine, straight, slanting lines; snow as flat round white dots and white caps on everything; wind as flowing hair, a streaming scarf and swirling leaves in whiplash curves; fog as flat pale bands; night as a deep blue-green sky with a pale gold moon and small stars.
Setting the way Mucha's posters have it, NOT a landscape or a city: there is no real place behind her at all. The halo, the weather's swirling ribbons and the plant ornament are the whole background, on flat cream paper or flat sky colour, and she stands on a simple, flat, stylised ground band of mosaic tiles or flowers. No canal, houses, bridge, water, street lamps, poles or trees.
