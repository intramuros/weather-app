Medium: an unmistakable 1930s Art Deco travel poster, like Cassandre's and the KLM and Holland-America Line posters: bold, simplified geometric shapes, strong symmetry and clean straight and curved edges, flat colour areas with smooth airbrushed gradients, crisp silhouettes, streamlined forms and long sunburst rays. Limited, sophisticated palette: deep navy, teal, cream, gold and ochre, coral red, black. A flat printed poster look with a faint lithograph grain. No pixel art, no anime shading, no photograph.
Keep the girl in her MODERN clothes from the reference, but draw her as an Art Deco poster figure, NOT anime: a strong, simplified silhouette made of a few geometric planes, crisp hard-edged light and shadow sides in flat colour, elegant elongated adult proportions, a calm, minimal face (simple almond eyes, no big eyes), and long chestnut hair as a few smooth, sculpted, geometric waves.
Weather the Art Deco way: sun as a gold disc with long, straight, fanning sunburst rays; clouds as stacked, streamlined horizontal bands; rain as long, perfectly straight, parallel slanting lines; snow as neat round white dots and smooth white caps on everything; wind as streamlined speed lines, flying hair, a scarf and leaves; fog as smooth airbrushed horizontal bands that hide the background; night as a deep navy airbrushed sky with a crisp gold crescent moon and small four-pointed stars.
The top third is a plain, smooth airbrushed sky gradient with nothing in it. Composition the travel-poster way, NOT a canal postcard: a low viewpoint so she stands tall and monumental like a poster figure, in strict symmetry; low and small behind her, a stylised harbour front with a streamlined ocean liner and a geometric brick Amsterdam School building, with flat, reflective water, all in the same flat geometric style. No gabled canal houses, no arched bridge, no flowers, no street lamps, poles or trees in the foreground. No text, lettering, title band or logo of any kind.
