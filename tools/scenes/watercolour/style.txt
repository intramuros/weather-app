Medium: a delicate, loose watercolour illustration on cold-pressed watercolour paper, like a fine children's picture book: transparent wet-in-wet washes, soft blooms and backruns, colour bleeding gently at the edges, granulating pigment, white paper left showing as highlights, and a few fine, light ink-pen contour lines on the girl only. Fresh, airy palette: cerulean and ultramarine blue, sap green, raw sienna, rose madder, warm grey, with lots of white paper. No pixel art, no anime cel shading, no digital gradients.
Keep the girl in her MODERN clothes from the reference, with a cute, gentle face, small dot eyes, rosy cheeks and long dark brunette hair, painted a little more crisply than the loose background so she reads at once.
Weather the watercolour way: sun as a soft warm yellow bloom with paper left white around it; clouds as wet grey-blue washes with soft edges; rain as fine, slanting dry-brush streaks and splashes on wet, reflective ground; snow as white paper dots left unpainted and masked-fluid flakes, white caps on everything; wind as streaked washes, flying hair, a scarf and leaves; fog as an almost-empty wash that dissolves the background into paper; night as a deep indigo wash with white paper stars and a pale moon.
The top third is a plain, pale wash fading to white paper. Composition the sketchbook way, NOT a full scene: a vignette that fades out into white paper towards every edge; only the wet or sunlit ground under her, her shadow or reflection, and a faint loosely washed hint of a gabled rooftop or two behind her; most of the paper stays white. No bridge, no flower beds, no street lamps.
